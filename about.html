About PetroPrime Fuels

Your trusted partner for petrol pump dealership and infrastructure consulting.

Who We Are

PetroPrime Fuels is a consultancy focused on the petrol pump and fuel retail sector. We assist entrepreneurs, landowners, and businesses in setting up new fuel retail outlets or upgrading existing facilities in line with oil marketing company and statutory norms.

With a strong understanding of industry guidelines, safety standards, and commercial considerations, we offer end-to-end project guidance and operational advisory.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify the complex journey of starting and running a petrol pump. We aim to ensure that our clients make informed decisions at every stage, resulting in sustainable, safe, and profitable fuel retail businesses.

  • Transparent, process-driven advisory
  • Focus on safety, compliance, and quality
  • Long-term partnership with our clients
